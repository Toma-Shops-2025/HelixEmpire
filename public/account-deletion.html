Delete Your Helix Empire Account

Helix Empire (package: online.viralsnap.helix) lets you request deletion of your account and associated data.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Helix Empire and sign in.
  2. Go to the WIN tab (Challenges).
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Delete by email

Email tomaaihelp@gmail.com from your registered address with the subject Helix Empire Account Deletion.

What we delete

  • Profile, username, and gameplay progress
  • JumpPoints, ViralCoins balance, and unlocked skins
  • Challenge and event participation tied to your account

Timeline

Deletion requests are processed within 30 days.

Contact

Questions: tomaaihelp@gmail.com
